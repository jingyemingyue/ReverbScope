ReverbScope <version>
Measure and compare the rooms you record in: reverberation, early reflections,
low-frequency resonances and noise, from any DAW.

  reverbscope demo                    Try it with synthetic data; no audio
                                    interface needed
  reverbscope gui                     Open the desktop app
  reverbscope sweep --out sweep.wav   Write the test signal to play from your DAW

Run reverbscope --help for every command and option.
